Profit ≠ cash.

When earnings grow, how much cash remains after capital spending? A source-linked comparison of Apple, Microsoft and Alphabet.

Research question → 24 curated filing facts → Python + SQL → six issuer-years → an exact cash-flow bridge.

Historical case, not a current market screen. Fiscal windows end in June, September and December; Apple's FY2023 has 53 weeks. Compare each issuer with its own prior year. Cross-issuer levels are not calendar-aligned.
